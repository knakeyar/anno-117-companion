### What Anno Companion uses

The import is intentionally pinned rather than following the calculator's moving default branch. [`tools/import_community_catalog.py`](tools/import_community_catalog.py) transforms the upstream structured data into [`catalog/anno117-community-2.1-c6a6e752.json`](catalog/anno117-community-2.1-c6a6e752.json). That catalog contains:

- 145 product references, including 113 factory input/output goods enabled for inventory telemetry;
- 144 factories and their input/output recipes and cycle times;
- region and DLC associations; and
- base maintenance and workforce requirements used only as clearly labeled planning estimates.

[`tools/import_population_planning.py`](tools/import_population_planning.py) separately extracts the pinned population levels, residence types and capacities, 125 per-residence need rates, and Low/Medium/High consumption factors into [`catalog/anno117-community-2.1-c6a6e752-planning.json`](catalog/anno117-community-2.1-c6a6e752-planning.json). The City Stock Planning table combines those catalog facts with observed city population, residence counts, factory counts, stock, and recipe cycles. These results are labeled as base planning estimates; observed net stock change remains a separate runtime measurement.

[`tools/generate_catalog.py`](tools/generate_catalog.py) validates those relationships and generates [`mod/anno-companion-telemetry/anno-companion/catalog.lua`](mod/anno-companion-telemetry/anno-companion/catalog.lua). The telemetry mod uses the generated product, factory, and residence GUID allowlists. The data service imports the same versioned facts, and the dashboard/API use them for names, coverage, recipe chains, workforce grouping, estimated base maintenance, city stock planning, inferred production pressure, and trade-planning context.

Anno Companion does not embed the calculator application or redistribute its game-icon files. The imported facts are combined with runtime observations produced by this project's telemetry mod; the resulting recommendations and current campaign state are generated by Anno Companion.
